# Governance

ScaleProof currently uses a **maintainer-led** model.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** merge PRs, accept or reject ADRs, cut releases, and handle security reports.
- **Contributors** are anyone who opens an issue or PR.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day decisions are made by PR review. One maintainer approval is required, or two for changes to public contracts or security-sensitive code.
- Architecture decisions are recorded as ADRs ([lifecycle](AGENTS.md#adr-lifecycle)). A Proposed ADR is open for comment for at least 7 days before it is accepted, unless it is urgent.
- Disagreements are resolved by maintainer consensus, or failing that, by a majority vote of maintainers.

This document will be revisited when there are three or more maintainers.
